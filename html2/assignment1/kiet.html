ABOUT KIET

KIET is a private university located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was established in 1998 and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of India. KIET offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in various fields of engineering, management, and applied sciences. The university has a strong focus on research and innovation, and it has collaborations with several international universities and research institutions. KIET is known for its state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and industry-oriented curriculum that prepares students for successful careers in their chosen fields.
